Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

5todos is designed with privacy as a priority. We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal personal identification to use our checklists.

2. Local Browser Storage

When you check off items on a checklist, your progress is saved locally inside your web browser using standard localStorage. This data remains on your physical device and is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

3. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Disclosure

Our checklists may contain outbound links to external source documents, official guidelines, or relevant product tools. Some outbound links may be affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, 5todos may earn a small referral commission at no additional cost to you.

4. Analytics & Cookies

We may use privacy-conscious analytics tools to measure basic aggregate usage metrics (such as total page visits and popular checklists) to help improve our service. We do not sell user data to third parties.

5. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or content accuracy, please reach out via our GitHub repository.
